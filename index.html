Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on the full 4 weeks of August

NEW: A DATE NIGHT 1G PREROLL BATCH NOW TESTS UNDER 20% THC

One of the two active Date Night 1g Preroll batches (C0140000239, 1,812u, 19.79% THC) is now flagged Restricted in this week's cultivation report — the first time a 1g Preroll SKU beyond the already-known Mint Condition/Homebody/Jai Alai trio has crossed the line. The other Date Night 1g Preroll split (21.83% THC) remains WS-eligible. Worth confirming with cultivation whether this is a one-off testing result or a trend for future Date Night batches, since Date Night is a top mover across both 7g Flower and 1g Prerolls.

FLOWER & PREROLL SUPPLY REMAINS DEEP — DEMAND, NOT PRODUCTION, IS THE LEVER

3.5g Flower, 7g Flower, and 1g Prerolls all closed August at 115-165% of their H2 Forecast targets while still carrying Overstocked Weeks of Supply (9.3, 8.6, and 35.4 respectively even before excluding restricted batches). The continued pause on new 1g Preroll production batches remains the right call; the more useful lever now is watching sell-through and SKU mix (Jai Alai and Date Night carried this week) rather than adding supply. 3.5g Flower's true WS-available figure (16,466u, excluding Bright Side) still comfortably covers demand.

0.5g 7pk PRJs — TRUE WS-AVAILABLE SUPPLY IS THE TIGHTEST SPOT IN THE PLAN

Headline Weeks of Supply reads Balanced (4.2), but with 4 of the category's 8 SKUs retail-only (Atomic Tangerine, Golden Hour, Mint Condition, Early Bird), true WS-available supply is roughly 2.1 weeks — the tightest true-supply position of any category, and it's the reason WS attainment closed the month at just 50.3% despite steady retail demand (64.7%, best of the constrained categories). Irish Goodbye finally showing real cultivation inventory (424u, resolving the zero-stock condition flagged in Weeks 1-3) is the one piece of good news; watch for whether the 6 batches Chris flagged as in labeling clear before mid-September.

AURA — RETAIL IS PULLING AHEAD OF CONFIRMED WS AVAILABILITY

Retail sold Sour Diesel (270u) and Pineapple Express (480u) this week even though WS orders only drew on the three previously-confirmed strains (Tahoe OG, Cake OG, Kush Mints) — worth confirming with Chris whether those two strains have in fact cleared brand registry for wholesale, since the Week 3 note left that as "hopeful, next week." If registry hasn't cleared, WS is leaving retail-validated demand on the table for two live SKUs.

1g VAPES (510) — DEMAND CAME BACK, BUT THE CATEGORY IS STILL THE FURTHEST BEHIND

WS Vape (510) rebounded to 180u after Week 3's zero week, but the category still closed August at just 28.1% of target — the weakest wholesale category in the plan alongside AURA (27.7%). The $27.50 WS / $55 retail price point flagged in Week 3 as above most of the competitive set (AGL, Rythm, Rodeo, Select, Loud, CTPharma) hasn't moved; a pricing or promo conversation for both Vape (510) and AURA remains worth having heading into September.

ACCOUNT NOTES — NO ACTION NEEDED

Clone Cannabis stayed within its agreed WS lane again this week (0.5g 7pk PRJs, Vape 510, and AURA only, across Meriden and Norwich) — no flower or 1g Preroll orders, consistent with their Springtime/Shaka arrangement. Zen Leaf, BUDR, and Curaleaf placed no WS orders this week; given their monthly budget caps, this reads as cap-driven rather than lost demand. HI! PEOPLE - Derby and The Harvest Corner - Colchester had no activity again this week (fourth straight week for Harvest Corner) — the formal tracked-account decision remains open and unchanged.

AUGUST CLOSES AT ~90% COMBINED — RETAIL 3.5g FLOWER IS THE CLEAREST GAP INTO SEPTEMBER

With all 4 weeks in, WS finished at 93.1% and Retail at 87.7% of their full Aug targets (Combined 90.5%) — both categories fell modestly short of July's ~117% WS / ~114% Retail closing pace. The gap is concentrated: Retail 3.5g Flower closed at just 47.7% of target (2,000 of 4,192u), the single largest shortfall in the plan, while WS Vape (510) and AURA remain the wholesale-side laggards. Worth flagging for the September plan rather than a mid-month correction, since the month is effectively closed.
