The stacking is concentrated: Umami OG 1g (3 batches, 8,147u), Jai Alai 1g (3 batches, 6,772u), Mint Condition 1g (2 batches, 6,547u), Cloud 9 1g (2 batches, 4,392u), and all three stacked vape strains above; 7g Early Bird and Homebody now each carry two batches as well. A suggested shape for the 2/4/6/8-month levers, each more aggressive than the last, for Chris to set:

  • 2 months: flag the batch, enforce first-in-first-out on the WS menu (hold newer batches of the same SKU off the menu until the older one drops below a set case count), and feature it in that week's outreach.
  • 4 months: a WS case discount or bundle attached to the aged batch, and a pause on releasing further batches of that SKU to on deck.
  • 6 months: a deeper markdown, an offer into our own retail stores through auto-clearance, and a production pause on the strain.
  • 8 months: liquidation pricing or rerouting (for example, Saus infusion feedstock where it fits spec), with no new production until the batch clears.

Cultivation is already applying the first 2-month lever informally: the newer Irish Goodbye and Jai Alai 1g Preroll batches are flagged Restricted until their prior batches sell through. Formalizing that as a separate FIFO-hold flag, distinct from the sub-20% retail-only restriction, would make the framework trackable in the weekly report. Two design notes. Batch Created may not be the right aging clock if a batch is created in the system well before it's packaged or tested, so it's worth confirming which date cultivation wants to count from. And mirroring the retail side's 30/60/90-day aging tags and SKU RAT flags from the S&OP review would let both channels run on one aging language. Right now the framework would flag roughly 18,790u (21% of cultivation stock) at the 2-month tier or older.
