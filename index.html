SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Week 1 of September

NEW: EARLY BIRD NOW HAS RESTRICTED BATCHES IN TWO CATEGORIES

Early Bird's 1g Preroll batch (C0140000242, 3,308u, 19.03% THC) is newly flagged Restricted this week — the first time this SKU has crossed the line for 1g Prerolls. Early Bird also has 2 batches of 0.5g 7pk Prerolls testing under 20% THC — only one (210u, 15.53% THC) currently shows in this week's cultivation report, with the second expected to post soon. Between the two categories, Early Bird is becoming a recurring THC-threshold risk — worth flagging to cultivation as a strain-level pattern rather than handling batch by batch.

0.5g 7PK PRJs — TRUE WS-AVAILABLE SUPPLY HAS CROSSED INTO UNDERSTOCKED TERRITORY, AND IT'S CONCENTRATED IN ONE SKU

Headline Weeks of Supply reads Balanced (3.7), but with 4 of the category's 8 SKUs retail-only (Atomic Tangerine, Early Bird, Golden Hour, Mint Condition), true WS-available supply is now roughly 1.6 weeks — below the 2-week Understocked threshold and down from ~2.1 weeks in August. Only 3 SKUs remain on the WS menu: Irish Goodbye (274u) and Umami OG (162u), both under 300u, and Couch Surfer, which carries 1,630u across three batches — 78.9% of the category's entire true WS-available stock. That concentration is before accounting for what will soon be 5 restricted batches held off the WS menu entirely for testing under 20% THC. This is the tightest true-supply position in the plan, and the SKU concentration compounds the risk — a single Couch Surfer supply issue would leave almost nothing else to sell.

1g VAPES (510) — WEAKEST WS OPENING WEEK ON RECORD FOR THIS CATEGORY

WS Vape (510) moved just 60u this week (2.3% of the September target) — only 2 cases, both from a single strain pair (Strawberry Cough, Blue Dream) at two accounts. Retail moved more volume in the same week (120u) than WS did, continuing the pattern from prior months where retail demand outpaces WS ordering for this category. Given the $27.50 WS / $55 retail price point has been flagged before as above much of the competitive set, this is worth an early pricing or promo conversation rather than waiting for a full month of soft numbers to confirm the trend.

AURA — ALL 6 STRAINS CONFIRMED BACK ON THE WS MENU FOR SEPTEMBER

Super Lemon Haze moved 210u at WS and 270u at retail this week, and Chris has confirmed all 6 AURA strains (Tahoe OG, Cake OG, Kush Mints, Sour Diesel, Pineapple Express, and Super Lemon Haze) are back available on both channels for the start of September — the first time the full lineup has been live simultaneously since the multi-week stockout. Worth watching whether WS attainment (26.7% of target in Week 1) starts closing the gap now that SKU availability is no longer the constraint.

ACCOUNT NOTES — WEEK 1 PACE CHECK

Zen Leaf ordered from 6 of its 7 CT locations this week for $25,800 total — only slightly above last month's spend pace, and well short of the $45k monthly spend Zen Leaf needs to reach (with payments on time) to unlock the agreed wholesale cost credits for their September promotion. A second round of orders to most locations before month-end will be needed to close that gap. BUDR and Crisp, both due for reorders, have been unresponsive; Affinity and Curaleaf, due for reorders in the near future, have also gone quiet despite in-store visits and direct outreach to purchasers. Crisp, Jananii, Rodeo, and Zen Leaf have all separately asked for wholesale support — either better upfront pricing or promotional support — that we're not in a position to offer yet until COGS come down enough to support healthier wholesale margins. The longer that gap persists, the more risk there is of accounts leaning on vendors who can offer promo support or price breaks now.

Separately: HI! PEOPLE - Hartford, a new account onboarded this week, appeared in WS data for the first time — same company group as HI! PEOPLE - Derby, onboarded a few months ago, just a different location. Shangri-La - Norwalk South is a separate, active wholesale account from Shangri-La - Norwalk Main, not a naming variant — both locations are now confirmed tracked accounts. One R&D/Infused PRJ sample order (120u, Fine Fettle - West Hartford) also appeared in retail data — a new-product test outside the standard 6 tracked categories, not included in totals above.
