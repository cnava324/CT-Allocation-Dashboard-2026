Section 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Weeks 1–3 of August

FLOWER INVENTORY EXTREMELY HEALTHY — WIDE VARIETY ACROSS 3.5g AND 7g

Cultivation is carrying 8 active 3.5g Flower SKUs (Mint Condition, Cloud 9, Date Night, Fritterz, Jai Alai, Middle Ground, Umami OG, Early Bird/Glow Up, plus the new Bright Side) and 6 active 7g Flower SKUs. Both categories remain comfortably Overstocked (8.2 and 9.3 weeks of supply respectively), but WS attainment is strong in both — 7g Flower is running at 142.5% of H2 Forecast MTD and 3.5g Flower has closed nearly all the way to target (94.9%) after a strong Week 3. This is the flower-variety-drives-pull-through pattern playing out as expected.

0.5g 7pk PRJs — LIMITED WS SKU OPTIONS, PIPELINE COMING

Only 6 SKUs are currently on the books for 0.5g 7pk PRJs, and 4 of them (Atomic Tangerine, Golden Hour, Mint Condition, Early Bird) are Restricted/retail-only — leaving true WS-available inventory around 1,569u against a 1,264u/week combined pace, much tighter than the headline 3.2-week Balanced signal implies. Per Chris's note, 6 additional batches are currently in labeling and 1 in testing — worth watching closely for when those clear, since WS 0.5g 7pk attainment is sitting at just 39.3% of H2 Forecast MTD and needs the SKU depth to catch up.

AURA — THIRD WS STRAIN LIVE, TWO MORE IN BRAND REGISTRY

Cake OG (2,028u) has been added as the third AURA strain on the wholesale menu alongside Tahoe OG and Kush Mints, and both channels continue to build momentum — WS moved 90u and Retail posted its best AURA week yet at 960u. Sour Diesel and Pineapple Express are currently passing through brand registry, with hope they clear and become available over the next week, which would bring the WS-available AURA lineup to 5 of 6 planned SKUs.

1g VAPES (510) & AURA DISPOSABLES — MARKET COMPRESSION PRESSURE ON PRICE POINT

WS Vape (510) sales dropped to zero this week (from 180u the week before) and remain the weakest wholesale performer at 21.1% of H2 Forecast MTD; AURA WS sits at 15.6% MTD, though retail AURA volume has been strong (960u this week). Broader market context applies to both categories: 1g 510-thread vapes, 1g distillate disposables, and live-resin vapes and disposables are showing up consistently on competitor retail menus (AGL, Rythm, Rodeo, Select, Loud, CTPharma, The Goods, Airo, Brix) priced between roughly $32–$50, while Fine Fettle's comffy Vape (510) sits at $27.50 WS / $55 retail and AURA sits at $30 WS / $60 MSRP — both above where the bulk of the competitive set has settled. A smaller group of brands (Tyson 2.0, Affinity, Loud, CTPharma) are still holding $55+ price points, suggesting a premium tier remains viable — but worth a pricing/promo conversation for both categories given where the broader market has compressed to.

1g PREROLLS — OVERSTOCK EASES SLIGHTLY BUT STAYS ELEVATED AT 34+ WEEKS

Weeks of supply came down modestly from 35.1 to 34.4 as sell-through outpaced new batch arrivals for the first time this month, but the category remains far and away the deepest overstock story. Demand continues to be strong in relative terms — 147.7% of H2 Forecast MTD, the best-performing WS category this week — reinforcing that this is a production/allocation question rather than a demand problem.

BRIGHT SIDE (3.5g FLOWER) — FIRST SUB-20% RESTRICTED BATCH IN THIS CATEGORY

A brand-new 3.5g Flower batch, Bright Side (3,857u, created 8/17, 18.98% THC), is confirmed retail-only per standing policy — any batch testing below 20% THC is restricted from the wholesale menu. This is the first time that's applied to a 3.5g Flower batch (previously only seen in 1g Prerolls and 0.5g 7pk PRJs); true WS-available 3.5g Flower is 13,759u rather than the 17,616u headline figure. No action needed — flagging just so the WS menu and Section 5 caveat stay consistent going forward.

HI! PEOPLE - DERBY AND THE HARVEST CORNER - COLCHESTER — NO ACTIVITY THIS WEEK, DECISION STILL OPEN

Neither account ordered in Week 3, so there's nothing new to weigh — the formal tracked-account decision carried from prior weeks remains unresolved.

MONTH-TO-DATE PACE CHECK

With 3 of 4 weeks in (~75% of the month elapsed), WS sits at 77.9% and Retail at 66.8% of their full Aug targets — WS is now running slightly ahead of the pace-line, Retail has closed most of the gap from last week but is still a bit behind. Combined attainment is 72.5%. One more strong week would put August in range of July's ~117% WS / ~114% Retail closing pace, though Retail will need the bigger push.
