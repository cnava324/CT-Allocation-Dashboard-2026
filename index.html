Section 6 — Strategic Observations

WinJuly closes at 117.4% of H2 Forecast — strongest month yet

WS finished all 5 weeks at 17,935 of the 15,276-unit July H2 Forecast target. 3.5g Flower (155.0%), 7g Flower (151.7%), and 1g Prerolls (159.0%) all closed the month well above target. Retail closed even stronger at 114.2% of its own target, with 1g Prerolls at retail finishing at 215.7% — the final week alone moved 3,090 retail preroll units, more than double any prior week this month. Combined, July finished at 115.8% of the blended target.

CriticalAURA — full stockout confirmed for the entire final week, third+ week running

The 7/29 cultivation inventory report has no AURA rows at all — no batches, no reserved quantity, nothing on order. Both WS and Retail moved zero units this week purely because there is nothing to sell. This has now been flagged for three consecutive weeks with no visible production movement in the data provided. Recommend escalating for a firm production/delivery date directly from cultivation — this is the one category actively costing WS+Retail revenue every week it stays out.

Watch3.5g and 7g Flower — Weeks of Supply dropped sharply on a huge sell-through week

3.5g Flower fell from 4.2 weeks of supply to 2.3, and 7g Flower from 3.8 to 2.6 — both still technically "Balanced" (above the <2-week Understocked line) but trending toward it fast. The drop is demand-driven, not a data issue: this week's combined WS+Retail sell-through (3,150u of 3.5g, 1,140u of 7g) tracks almost exactly to the drop in available inventory, confirming the report is current. Both categories carry the largest of the invoice-confirmed Reserved/On Order units (Fine_F-459/460), which helps, but worth confirming the next production batch timeline before these cross into Understocked territory.

Ongoing1g Prerolls — still severely overstocked at 28.3 weeks of supply despite a record demand week

Even with 7,571 combined units moving this week alone — the single biggest weekly pull of any category all month — 1g Prerolls remain the clear outlier at 28.3 weeks of supply against a 4,072-unit combined monthly target. This is consistent with the ongoing demand-signal story (inventory isn't the constraint), but the gap is now large enough that even sustained record weeks like this one barely move the needle. The discount/loyalty initiative status check flagged in prior weeks is still the open lever here.

Improving1g Vapes (510) — Weeks of Supply easing back toward Balanced

Weeks of Supply came down from 7.4 to 6.9 — still technically Overstocked but the smallest gap over the 6-week threshold in recent weeks. WS closed the month at only 52.2% of its H2 Forecast target, but Retail closed at 161.1%, so the category's overall demand pull is stronger than the WS-only number suggests.
