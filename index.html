1g VAPES (510) — BEST WS WEEK OF THE MONTH IN THE FINAL TWO DAYS

WS moved 210u of vapes on 9/29 (Affinity both locations, RISE both locations), more than the first four weeks combined, bringing September to 390u (14.7% of target). Since the $26.00 price went live, WS has taken 240u in two deliveries versus 150u across the three weeks before it. Much of that came from Affinity's reactivation, so it's too early to credit the price alone, but it's the first positive read. Headline supply sits at 6,882u (8.6 weeks), and Purple Urkle (866u) is now about 188 days old. The retail shelf gap (competitor vapes at $40-45 versus $52-55 for Comffy and Aura) still stands heading into October.

7g FLOWER AND 0.5g 7PK PRJs — THE TWO CATEGORIES TO WATCH GOING INTO OCTOBER

7g Flower fell to 4.8 weeks of supply, the lowest of the month, after 1,455u moved in the final week across both channels. The S&OP review already flagged 7g as the tightest carried-in buffer (56 days of cover). The 7-pack's sellable supply dropped back to about 1.8 weeks after the largest retail delivery of the month, and more than half of category stock is still restricted to retail. Both are worth raising on Friday's stage-gate call before October menus go out.

SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Month-end read · forward-looking considerations for the October build

SEPTEMBER CLOSE: UNITS ON PLAN, DOLLARS AT 74% OF PLAN

WS finished at 14,696u (89.9% of target) and $237,767 (74.3% of the $320K plan month). Against August, units were flat (-0.1%) and dollars rose 4.8%. The $82.4K revenue gap to plan comes mostly from three categories: Vapes (510) $62.4K under target revenue, AURA $25.6K under, and 0.5g 7pk PRJs $10.9K under, with 3.5g Flower another $6.2K short. The two categories that beat target, 7g Flower (+$15.7K) and 1g Prerolls (+$7.1K), couldn't offset that at their lower dollars per unit. Retail was the opposite story at 227.6% of target.

OCTOBER FORECAST INPUTS: SEVERAL SEPTEMBER TARGETS LOOK MISCALIBRATED

The October WS existing-SKU targets step up about 3% from September (16,892u), with the plan calling for $402K once new SKUs are included. Three September results suggest the targets underneath need a look first. 7g Flower and 1g Prerolls beat WS target by 33% and 66%. On the retail side they ran at 218% and 573%, which suggests the flat-vs-August retail demand line is understated. Vapes (510) delivered 390u against a 2,645u WS target, roughly 15% attainment, its second straight month under 30% (August closed at 28.1%, July at 52.2%). Unless the $26.00 price and the Aura cart launch change the trend quickly, the Comffy cart WS demand line (2,733u for October) looks overstated. This connects to the open question of keeping Comffy vapes in wholesale or making them retail-exclusive as Aura takes over.

BUYER BREADTH: 14 GROUPS AND 39 DOORS ORDERED IN SEPTEMBER, BUT SEVERAL WENT QUIET

Across the month, 14 buyer groups and 39 unique doors placed at least one WS order. Because 39 already exceeds the ~30-door December target on a monthly-unique basis, it's worth confirming how that target is measured (for example, doors ordering each week or doors stocking a full SKU set). Accounts that went a full month or more without an order, per the month-end buyer performance report: all four Shangri-La locations (30 to 57 days), Clone Cannabis - Meriden and Norwich (40 days), Rodeo - Rocky Hill (44 days), BUDR - Tolland and West Hartford (58 days), and Zen Leaf - Ashford (77 days). Crisp (25-26 days) and THE LIV - Putnam (27 days) are due on their monthly cadence. HI! PEOPLE - Hartford reordered on 9/28, and Affinity's 9/29 delivery brought both locations back.

ZEN LEAF AND RECEIVABLES AT MONTH-END

Zen Leaf closed September at $25,800 across 6 locations with no second round, short of the $45K threshold for the agreed wholesale cost credits. On the positive side, it has paid its September invoices ahead of the 10/3 due date, with only $825.72 open at Meriden, which keeps the relationship in good standing for an October reset. Overdue AR stands at 8 invoices and $37,946. Nearly half is RISE - Branford ($9,407) and RISE - Orange ($9,009), both just 4 days past due. The older items are Crisp - Cromwell (2 invoices, $4,755, due 9/5 and 9/10), Higher Collective - Torrington ($2,910, due 9/6), and Crisp - East Hartford ($2,625, due 9/6), plus Clone Cannabis - Meriden ($5,175) and Norwich ($4,065), both due 9/24. BUDR's overdue invoices from last week have cleared. Total open WS AR is $249,227 across 56 invoices.

AGING-INVENTORY FRAMEWORK: MONTH-END BASELINE

By Batch Created date, 17,049u (20% of cultivation stock) is 2 months or older at month-end, down from about 18,790u last week: 2-4 months 14,391u (1g Prerolls 11,631u, 0.5g 7pk 1,599u, Vapes 945u, 3.5g Flower 216u); 4-6 months 1,792u (Jai Alai 1g, ~146 days); 6-8 months 866u (Purple Urkle vape, ~188 days); 8+ months none. On the current path, Jai Alai 1g crosses the 6-month tier in early November and Purple Urkle crosses the 8-month tier around late November. Setting the tier levers and a separate FIFO-hold flag before the October build would let the framework start working on those two batches before they cross.

LOW-POTENCY PRE-ROLL PRICING: WHERE IT STANDS

Higher Collective - Torrington remains the only account to take the low-potency 1g Prerolls at $4.75 (3 cases). The proposed Torrington tiers ($4.40 at 8 cases, $4.00 at 14 cases, each holding gross profit per order at or above today's order) are still pending. Two items to settle before offering them: confirm with finance whether the $5.12 floor in the pricing note or the approved $4.75 Q4 list governs, and clear Torrington's $2,910 overdue invoice.

INTO OCTOBER

The October WS menu carries the Q4 price list and five new products: 10g Smalls, Saus singles (multipacks later in the month), the Comffy cart flavor refresh, Aura 1g carts, and Aura 2g disposables. Infused flower and BiteBack gummies follow in November and December. The plan asks WS to grow from September's $237,767 to $402K in October. The 10/1 Harvest Corner delivery (195u) is the first October order on the books.
