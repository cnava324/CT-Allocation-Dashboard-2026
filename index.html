WEEK 2: HOLIDAY-SHORT WEEK; $133.9K MTD, 44.6% OF $300K

Veterans Day closed deliveries Wednesday and Thursday, so Week 2 was the Tuesday 11/10 run only: $26,092.50 across 6 doors (RISE, Affinity, HC Bridgeport and Torrington). MTD WS is $133,868.50, 44.6% of the $300K goal with 46.7% of the month gone, and Q4 to date is $338,282 (37.6% of $900K). The 11/17 run has $40,132.50 confirmed. If the $37,542 of held orders also clear, November reaches $211,543 (70.5%) on 11/17, leaving about $88K for the last two delivery weeks, one of them Thanksgiving week.

ORDER CUTOFFS: $57.6K OF ORDERS MISSED MONDAY’S DEADLINE

BUDR ($27,698), Zen Leaf ($26,993) and The Harvest Corner ($2,903) submitted after the Monday 10am cutoff despite reminders, which is why 11/17 is heavy. Thanksgiving week will lose Thursday and likely Friday deliveries, and Green Wednesday is 11/25, so that week’s cutoff matters more than usual. Publishing a Thanksgiving-week schedule now (orders by Friday 11/20 for Tuesday 11/24 delivery), plus a Friday reminder and a Monday 8am final call, would protect it. For BUDR and Zen Leaf, which reorder on a regular cycle, a pre-built suggested cart sent Friday that the buyer only has to approve would remove most of the delay.

AR HOLDS: ZEN LEAF IS THE SWING ($28.3K PAST DUE, $27.0K HELD)

Overdue WS AR is $48,977 across 13 invoices, up from $16,996 on 11/4, even though Clone Cannabis ($13,470) and THE LIV ($2,700.15) paid in full. The increase is Zen Leaf’s 10/8 cycle (due 11/7) and Shangri-La’s 10/13 cycle ($17,513), which came due 11/12. Zen Leaf, HC Torrington and INSA New Haven hold $37,542 of 11/17 orders between them. Escalating Zen Leaf to their accounts-payable contact early next week, and writing down one rule that Lauren and the reps apply the same way (orders hold at 1 day past due and release when the payment posts), would shorten these cycles. Shangri-La is worth a courtesy reminder before its next order, given the holiday timing.

CASE SIZES: A 15-UNIT HALF CASE FOR SMALLER BUYERS

Sweetspot’s new purchaser asked for 15-20 unit cases, so a test order can cover 4 strains instead of 2 without cash sitting in slow inventory. The same applies to Crisp, Rodeo, The Harvest Corner and THE LIV, whose orders run $2-4K per store. Listing a 15-unit half case as its own APEX variant on 3.5g, 7g and 7-packs would test this without new packaging. The costs are extra pick-and-pack time and Metrc package splits, so a pilot through December with Sweetspot and two or three small accounts, with a minimum order size, would show whether wider variety lifts reorder rates enough to keep it.

PROMO REQUESTS: TIE DISCOUNTS TO AGED BATCHES

Three discount asks arrived together: RISE’s aging-inventory credit, HI! PEOPLE’s 20% Green Wednesday support on comffy and AURA, and competing vendors (Curaleaf, Affinity, CTPharma) offering 20-40% off wholesale. One December offer on batches 4+ months old (6,398u) would answer all three without discounting new strains that sell through at list price: Mint Condition 1g (3,013u, 136 days), Cloud 9 1g (903u, 147 days), Jai Alai 1g (650u, 190 days), Purple Urkle vape (536u, 232 days) and the $700 Wedding Cake vape batch (535u, 140 days). For RISE, a credit against its own aged sell-through keeps list pricing intact. For HI! PEOPLE, funding the 20% as a capped credit on the next invoice, limited to Green Wednesday week, keeps the promo measurable.
