WEEK 1: $107.8K, 35.9% OF THE $300K GOAL

November opened with $107,776 across 17 invoices and 16 doors, the strongest first week of Q4 (October Week 1 was $82,688). Q4 to date is $312,189, 34.7% of $900K. To reach $300K, the remaining 3.3 weeks need about $58K per week; the $348K catch-up pace needs about $73K per week. Only $7,920 is confirmed for Week 2 so far (HC Bridgeport and HC Torrington; THE LIV stays on hold), so the mid-month restock round from BUDR, Curaleaf, Zen Leaf, and High Profile (none ordered in Week 1) is the swing factor.

7g FLOWER: WS DEMAND OUTRAN MENU STOCK

WS took 1,530u of 7g in Week 1 (62% of the November target) and retail took 3,120u, which emptied Brainstorm, Moon Lander, and Glow Up. Sellable 7g is now 1,189u (Early Bird and Super Chill), under one week of combined pace, with 2,269u of Early Bird on deck. Moving that batch to the menu and setting a WS share before the mid-month round would keep 7g, last month’s best WS category, from stalling.

CONCENTRATION: TWO GROUPS DROVE 58% OF WEEK 1

Higher Collective ($34,522 across 3 doors) and Shangri-La ($27,758 across 4 doors, Waterbury back after skipping October) made up 58% of Week 1. Higher Collective - Bridgeport alone was $23,541. That’s strong account depth, but it puts weight on two buyers; Hamden (HC) and both Affinity doors are the nearest wins for breadth.

AURA: 2g OPENS TO WS; 1g MENU STOCK IS THIN

AURA 2g came off restriction and HC Bridgeport’s 4 cases (11/10) are the first WS order. Sellable 2g is 1,298u against a combined November target of 1,424u, and retail already took 1,290u in Week 1, so a WS case cap per door would spread the launch. AURA 1g sellable stock is 1,334u on the menu, with 3,958u on deck; releasing one on-deck batch keeps AURA 1g open while stores shift toward 2g.

VAPES: RETAIL EXCLUSIVITY WINDOWS SET THE WS RAMP

WS vapes are at 4.3% of target because all four Comffy refresh flavors (6,622u) and all four new AURA carts (7,365u) are retail-only. The Comffy refresh hit stores 10/28 and the AURA carts 11/4, so under the 2-week window they open to WS around 11/11 and 11/18. Putting those dates in front of buyers now would let Week 3 and Week 4 orders cover the 2,820u vape and 452u AURA cart targets.

7-PACKS: NEW STRAINS SOLD THROUGH IN ONE WEEK

Airplane Mode and Brainstorm 7-packs went from 299u and 266u to below one case each. WS-eligible stock is 889u (Sweet Tooth and Couch Surfer) plus 658u Couch Surfer on deck, while 1,037u sits in four sub-20% retail-only batches. Retail took only 296u, so stores aren’t competing for this stock; the next Airplane Mode or Brainstorm run is the WS growth lever.

RETAIL: 77.2% OF THE 10-STORE TARGET IN ONE RESTOCK

Using targets scaled to 10 stores (16,504u existing SKUs), the 11/4 restock reached 77.2%, with 3.5g, 7g, and 1g pre-rolls already above the full month. AURA 1g (11.2%) and 7-packs (8.5%) lag as stores move to the AURA 2g and the new carts. The forecast sheet itself still carries flat 9-store numbers, so updating it, with AURA 2g and carts absorbing part of AURA 1g demand, would make the December plan more reliable.

AR: OVERDUE DOWN TO $17.0K; $111K DUE BY 11/14

Overdue WS AR is $16,996 across 5 invoices, down from $20,079 on 10/29; HC Torrington cleared its $3,082.65. Clone Cannabis is $13,470 of it (Norwich and Meriden at 41 days). Another $111,430 across 37 invoices comes due 11/4-11/14, mostly the October Week 1-2 cycle (Zen Leaf, BUDR, Curaleaf, High Profile). Total open WS AR is $312,213 across 80 invoices.

AGING: 32% OF STOCK IS 2+ MONTHS OLD

By Batch Created date, 31,541u (32% of 97,819u) is 2+ months old: 2-4 months 24,243u (1g Prerolls 16,448u), 4-6 months 5,872u (Mint Condition 1g 3,373u at 127 days, 7-packs 742u, Wedding Cake vape 595u), 6-8 months 1,426u (Jai Alai 1g 740u crossed 6 months 11/3; Purple Urkle vape 686u crosses 8 months around 11/21). Leading Mint Condition and Jai Alai 1g in Week 2 outreach, and offering Purple Urkle at the $700 legacy price, would move the oldest stock first.
