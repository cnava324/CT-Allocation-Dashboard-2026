SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Week 2 of September

NEW: MIDDLE GROUND 1G PREROLL BATCH NOW TESTS UNDER 20% THC

A Middle Ground 1g Preroll batch (1,023u, 15.75% THC) is newly flagged Restricted this week — the first time this SKU has crossed the line for 1g Prerolls. This is now the sixth strain-batch combination restricted in this category (alongside Date Night, Early Bird, Homebody, and two Jai Alai batches), pushing true WS-available 1g Preroll inventory down to 29,922u against a 46,747u headline. Worth raising with cultivation as a category-wide pattern rather than a one-off.

0.5g 7PK PRJs — LAST WEEK'S RELIEF WAS SHORT-LIVED

True WS-available Weeks of Supply recovered to ~2.7 weeks after last week's new batches (Cloud 9, Super Chill, Glow Up), but strong retail demand pulled 1,263u of the category this week alone — those same three batches were retail's top movers. True WS-available supply is back down to ~1.9 weeks, below the 2-week Understocked line again. This category is proving to be the most demand-sensitive in the plan; worth discussing whether WS ordering should be actively encouraged before retail absorbs the next batch too.

1g VAPES (510) — A DEMAND AND PRICING PROBLEM, NOT A SUPPLY ONE; PRODUCTION PAUSE UNDER CONSIDERATION

Supply hasn't been the constraint here for months. A Blue Dream batch that had run down to 3 cases (from 10 a few weeks ago) got a well-timed replacement this week — a new batch went RFS On Deck right as the old one was running out. The rest of the comffy vape lineup still holds 15–30 cases per batch with backup batches coming behind. The real issue is a severe, sustained dip in comffy vape sales, compounded by pricing: at $55/g wholesale, we're well above the $40–50/g most competitors are charging in a market that's seeing a broader vape pricing pullback. A pricing update is in active discussion alongside the upcoming AURA vape rollout. Worth considering pausing comffy vape production until current inventory clears — ahead of introducing the Fruit Forward terpenes/names — and revisiting whether comffy vapes stay in wholesale distribution at all, versus going retail-exclusive and letting AURA disposables take over wholesale vape demand as that line scales.

RETAIL IS PULLING FAR AHEAD OF WHOLESALE THIS MONTH

Retail sits at 81.6% of its full September target after just 2 of ~4–5 weeks, against WS at 40.3% — a wider gap than any month tracked so far. 1g Prerolls at retail is already at 180.8% of its monthly target combined, consistent with the ongoing overstock-liquidation pattern. Worth flagging for the October forecast: if this pace holds, retail targets may need to move up and WS targets may need a harder look at what's actually limiting wholesale ordering beyond supply.

ACCOUNT NOTES — WEEK 2 PACE CHECK

Crisp placed its usual-sized reorder this week (~$3k per location across all 3 locations) — normal monthly cadence, not a turnaround from an unresponsive stretch — but leaned heavily into 1g Prerolls over 3.5g Flower and comffy Vapes. Chris reached out to add AURA disposables to the order since Crisp has sold off their AURA menu entirely, but got no response yet; hoping the next reorder comes back flower-heavy (prerolls have been outselling flower there) and includes AURA.

THE LIV - Putnam placed their AURA reorder ahead of a wholesale support call with purchaser Jonny at the end of last week. Jonny asked for promotional support to bring their margins to a full 50%; Chris explained we can't cover a full promotion's cost right now, but floated splitting promotional costs in the future once comffy flower SKUs are back on shelves with more consistency — that consistency is the prerequisite for offering more wholesale support. Owner Hem, via Jonny, pushed back wanting better front-line pricing to lean into our flower SKUs again; Chris held firm that's not an option right now, noting our flower is already the most competitively priced in the category, while prerolls and vapes run slightly higher than value brands.
