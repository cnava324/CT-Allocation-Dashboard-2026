SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Weeks 1-2 of October

WEEK 2: $56.6K DELIVERED; THE $300K OCTOBER GOAL NOW NEEDS ABOUT $80K A WEEK

Week 2 delivered 4,185u and $56,553 to 22 doors, bringing October to 9,224u and $139,241: 46.4% of the $300K goal with 55% of the month elapsed (17 of 31 days), and 15.5% of the $900K Q4 goal. Reaching $300K needs $160,759 across the 10/24 and 10/31 weeks, about $80.4K a week, or roughly 5,300u a week at October's average of about $15 a unit. That is a bit above Week 1 (5,039u, $82,688), the best WS week since early August. No orders for 10/19 onward were in APEX when the reports ran, so next week's pipeline is the number to watch. If October lands short, November and December would need to average above $300K to hold the $900K Q4 goal.

NEW WS PRICE LIST: ABOUT 4% LESS REVENUE PER UNIT ON OCTOBER'S MIX

The 10/15 inventory report carries the new list for deliveries from 10/19: core 3.5g Flower $14.00 (from $15.00-$16.00), 1g Prerolls $4.75 (from $5.50-$6.50) with low-potency at $4.00 (from $4.75), and 0.5g 7pk $17.00 (from $18.00-$22.00). 7g Flower holds at $22.00 (Early Bird rises from $20.00), and vapes, AURA, and SAUS are unchanged. Repricing October's paid WS units at the new list gives about $5.3K less (3.8%): 1g Prerolls down 18%, 7-packs down 11%, 3.5g down 6%. The cuts land on the categories with the most stock (3.5g at 9.8 weeks, 1g Prerolls at 33.8 weeks), so units need to rise by about the same share just to hold dollars. Tracking units per account in the two weeks after the change against Weeks 1-2 would show whether the lower price is pulling volume.

ACCOUNTS: CURALEAF, SHANGRI-LA, AND HI! PEOPLE - DERBY ORDERED; 32 DOORS SO FAR

Curaleaf reordered at Groton ($5,895), Manchester ($2,955), and Stamford ($2,505); Meriden hasn't ordered in October yet. Shangri-La returned at all four locations ($17,513), the largest group order of the week, and HI! PEOPLE - Derby added $4,665. Torrington's order slipped one day to 10/16. Zen Leaf didn't order in Week 2 and is still about $12.1K short of the $45K October credit threshold with two weeks left. October now has 32 doors across 10 buyer groups, against 39 doors for all of September. Not yet ordering in October: Crisp (3), Affinity (2), Clone Cannabis, Higher Collective (Bridgeport, New London, Hamden), HI! PEOPLE - Hartford, THE LIV - Putnam, Rodeo, and Curaleaf - Meriden.

SAUS: SINGLES NEARLY SOLD THROUGH IN TWO WEEKS

SAUS 1g singles moved 2,044u in two weeks: 1,219u to Fine Fettle stores and 825u to WS, where RISE (2 locations) and BUDR (7 locations) were the only accounts allocated (660u paid plus 165u of samples). Blue Dream and Tahoe OG are sold out at cultivation and 357u of J. Fruit remains, against a combined October target of 580u. With multipacks planned as a limited WS release, setting the allocation rule now (which accounts qualify, and a case cap per door) would keep the next batches from going first-come, and would tell accounts like Zen Leaf, Curaleaf, and Shangri-La when to expect access.

COMFFY VAPES: WS IS OUT-PULLING RETAIL ABOUT 3 TO 1 IN OCTOBER

October Comffy 510 vapes went 390u to WS accounts and only 120u to Fine Fettle stores, the reverse of September (390u WS, 1,129u retail). Berry Grape and Lemon Cake are on hold until the end of October to align with the planned retail release, and several batches of the older flavors will transition out once depleted. That legacy stock is 7,326u (Wedding Cake 2,540u, Blue Dream 2,323u, Strawberry Cough 1,717u, Purple Urkle 746u) and moved about 255u a week across both channels in October, about 29 weeks of supply. With WS currently the stronger vape channel, a defined WS release trigger for the refresh (a unit floor per legacy strain, a fixed date, or pairing a refresh case with legacy cases for accounts that reorder vapes, such as High Profile, INSA, and Zen Leaf - Meriden) would keep the new flavors from waiting into Q2. Purple Urkle (203 days old) moved 0u this week and crosses 8 months around 11/21.

NEW PRODUCTS: TWO-WEEK RETAIL EXCLUSIVITY BEFORE WS

New products will launch to Fine Fettle stores two weeks before WS. The H2 WS forecast doesn't reflect that window yet: AURA 2g (463u) and 10g Smalls (425u) carry October WS targets with no stock in the 10/15 report, so the 1,317u October new-product WS target will likely be met only through SAUS, which already delivered 825u. Shifting each new product's WS forecast start two weeks behind its retail launch would keep the targets honest and stop new products from reading as behind pace on the dashboard.
