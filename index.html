SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Week 1 of October

WEEK 1: STRONGEST WS WEEK SINCE EARLY AUGUST, AHEAD OF UNIT PACE, SLIGHTLY BEHIND THE DOLLAR GOAL

Week 1 delivered 5,039u, the highest weekly WS total since August Week 1 (7,110u) and above every September week (best was 3,926u), though it spans 10/1-10/9 and includes the carried-over Harvest Corner order. Units are at 29.8% of the existing-SKU target with 32% of the month elapsed. Dollars trail slightly: $82,688 is 27.6% of the $300K October goal ($900K for Q4), which leaves about $72.4K a week for the remaining three weeks. The 10/13-10/15 pipeline already holds $40,533, so Week 2 needs roughly $32K more in new orders to stay on pace. The H2 forecast's revenue targets in Section 1 ($330,744 existing SKUs, $369,052 with new products) now sit above the $300K goal, so the $300K goal is the dollar benchmark to track against.

ZEN LEAF AND BUDR REORDERED ACROSS THEIR ROSTERS; SHANGRI-LA RETURNS NEXT WEEK

Zen Leaf ordered at all 7 locations ($32,881), including Ashford after 77 days without an order, which leaves about $12.1K to reach the $45K October threshold for the agreed cost credits. BUDR reordered at 6 locations ($18,300), bringing Tolland and West Hartford back after 58 days, and has SAUS preorders at all 7 locations for 10/13. High Profile - Stratford placed the week's largest order ($10,088). Shangri-La is in the 10/13 pipeline at all four locations after a month or more without an order. Not yet ordering in October: Curaleaf, Crisp, Affinity, HI! PEOPLE, Higher Collective (Torrington is in the 10/15 pipeline), Clone Cannabis, THE LIV - Putnam, and Rodeo.

VAPES: BEST WS START OF H2 AT $26.00, BUT CONCENTRATED IN ONE ACCOUNT

Week 1 moved 330u of Comffy 510 vapes, 85% of September's full-month total, but 240u came from High Profile - Stratford, so it's too early to call a trend. Supply is 9,437u (11.5 weeks) including the new Berry Grape flavor batch on hold. Pairing the flavor refresh with the accounts that just reordered (High Profile, Zen Leaf, INSA, which has 60u in the 10/13 pipeline) gives the category its best chance to keep this pace. Retail took only 30u this week.

SAUS LAUNCH: RETAIL IS LIVE AND WS PREORDERS ALREADY EXCEED THE OCTOBER TARGET

SAUS Blue Dream and Tahoe OG reached all 9 Fine Fettle stores on 10/7 (540u). The WS preorder holds 660 paid units for 10/13 and 10/15 across BUDR's 7 locations and both RISE locations, already 154% of the 429u October WS target before any other account has ordered. After those orders, 1,036u is unreserved. Deciding now how much of it goes to the accounts that asked about SAUS on field visits (Zen Leaf, Higher Collective - New London, Crisp, High Profile, Clone, Curaleaf - Manchester) and how much stays for retail reorders would avoid a first-come shortfall, especially for accounts still on order hold. Section 5 reads SAUS as Overstocked only because the October targets (580u combined) predate launch; retail alone took 540u in its first delivery.

AR: OVERDUE IS DOWN TO $12.6K; ZEN LEAF - MERIDEN SHIPPED WITH A SMALL OPEN BALANCE

As of 10/8, overdue WS AR is 4 invoices and $12,645.87, down from $28,170 on 10/2. Higher Collective (Torrington and Bridgeport), Crisp - East Hartford, INSA - Hartford, and both Shangri-La invoices due 10/3 have cleared, so Torrington's 10/15 order and Shangri-La's 10/13 orders can ship. Still open: Clone Cannabis - Meriden ($5,175) and Norwich ($4,065), 14 days past due; HI! PEOPLE - Hartford ($2,580.15, 7 days); and Zen Leaf - Meriden ($825.72, 5 days), which received a 10/8 order while that balance was open. That's a small exception to the net-30 order hold worth confirming, in case the balance is tied to the September promo credit. Ten September invoices ($39,354) come due 10/10, including RISE ($12,654) and all 3 Crisp locations. Total open WS AR is $270,176 across 76 invoices, including the 10/13-10/15 pipeline.

AGING INVENTORY: TWO BATCHES APPROACH THE NEXT TIER IN NOVEMBER

By Batch Created date, 19,464u (21% of cultivation stock) is 2 months or older, up from 17,049u at month-end as August batches crossed 60 days: 2-4 months 17,286u (1g Prerolls 12,212u, 7g Flower Early Bird 2,480u, 0.5g 7pk 1,419u, Vapes 855u, 3.5g Flower 216u, AURA 104u); 4-6 months 1,432u (Jai Alai 1g, 154 days); 6-8 months 746u (Purple Urkle vape, 196 days). Jai Alai moved 360u across both channels this week but still crosses 6 months around 11/3, and Purple Urkle crosses 8 months around 11/21. Purple Urkle moved 120u this week; at that pace it clears in about 6 weeks, so a targeted push in the next two weeks would finish it before the 8-month mark.

LOW-POTENCY PRE-ROLLS AND TORRINGTON

Only one low-potency 1g case moved in WS this week (High Profile - Stratford), while retail took 360u of Early Bird. Higher Collective - Torrington, now clear of its overdue invoice, has 5 low-potency cases in the 10/15 pipeline at $4.75, short of the proposed 8-case tier at $4.40. That makes the 8-case tier a realistic next step for its November order, once finance confirms whether the $5.12 floor or the approved $4.75 Q4 list governs.
