1g VAPES (510) — GROWING INVENTORY, ZERO WS ORDERS, AND A COMPETITIVENESS GAP THAT OCTOBER PRICING MAY NOT CLOSE

One new batch landed this week — Strawberry Cough (1,786u, non-restricted, on deck) — the third large Vapes batch to land in two weeks, pushing headline availability to 7,650u (9.6 weeks of supply, Overstocked). Yet WS placed zero orders again this week, the category's second zero-order week of the month. The October price cut to $26 wholesale helps, but the bigger concern is retail-side competitiveness: market compression has several vape options on shelves statewide at $40-45 retail with meaningfully higher sell-through, while comparable Comffy/Aura vape products are retailing at $52-55. Closing the wholesale gap without closing the retail gap may not be enough to restart demand — worth pushing for a harder look at shelf pricing, not just the wholesale list, before the October menus lock.

SECTION 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Week 3 of September

NEW: SEPTEMBER IS TRACKING WELL AHEAD OF PLAN — 1g PREROLLS ALREADY PAST FULL-MONTH TARGET

1g Prerolls (110.0%) has exceeded its full-month WS target, and combined (WS+Retail) it sits at 191.4% — retail alone is at 287.8% for that category. 7g Flower came close (98.8%) but landed just under after two corrections this week; see below. Retail overall is at 115.5% of its full monthly target after just 3 weeks. This is the strongest pace of any month tracked so far and is largely demand-driven rather than supply-driven (inventory for these categories remains Overstocked). Worth flagging now for the October forecast conversation rather than waiting for month-end — both the WS and combined targets for 1g Prerolls, and likely 7g Flower too, probably need to move up.

0.5g 7PK PRJs — STILL THE TIGHTEST CATEGORY IN THE PLAN

True WS-available Weeks of Supply slipped further this week to roughly 1.5 — its worst reading yet, down from ~1.9 last week and ~2.7 the week before that. The category has now spent 2 of the last 3 weeks below the 2-week Understocked line despite two rounds of new batch additions. This category's WS menu remains structurally thin (only 3-5 of 8 SKUs available at any time) and is the most consistent supply risk in the plan; worth a dedicated conversation with cultivation on a standing buffer rather than reactive batch-by-batch additions.

MAJOR ACCOUNT REACTIVATIONS: BUDR AND CURALEAF BOTH RETURNED THIS WEEK

BUDR (all 5 locations) and Curaleaf (3 of 4 locations delivered this week, with Meriden's order rescheduled to 9/22 — see below) — both flagged as dormant for weeks, with Curaleaf having gone dark for all of August — placed large orders across their full rosters, driving most of the week's WS volume in 3.5g Flower, 7g Flower, and 1g Prerolls. THE HARVEST CORNER - Colchester, previously an open tracked-account decision, has now ordered consistently for two weeks running (90u 3.5g Flower, 90u 7g Flower, 60u 0.5g 7pk PRJs this week) — worth formalizing its tracked status given the sustained activity.

TWO DELIVERY CORRECTIONS THIS WEEK: CURALEAF - MERIDEN RESCHEDULED, INSA - NEW HAVEN SHORT-SHIPPED

Curaleaf - Meriden's delivery, originally scheduled for 9/17 as part of this week's broader Curaleaf reactivation, was rejected at the door due to an internet outage at the new store that knocked out the security cameras. The order (120u 3.5g Flower, 60u 7g Flower, 90u 1g Prerolls — 270u total, $3,705 in revenue) has been rescheduled for Tuesday 9/22 and now shows up in next week's pipeline instead of this week's Section 1 totals. Separately, INSA - New Haven was short-shipped on its 9/17 delivery — 30u of Irish Goodbye 7g Flower delivered against 60u requested ($660 in revenue). Between the two corrections, 7g Flower's initial read of having exceeded its full-month target flipped to landing just under (98.8%) — the volume itself hasn't disappeared, but this week's numbers now reflect what was actually delivered rather than what was originally scheduled.

NEW DATA SOURCES: BUYER PERFORMANCE AND AR REPORTS SURFACE A FEW ITEMS WORTH FOLLOWING UP

The buyer performance report shows three accounts have gone quiet since their opening September order and haven't reordered since: HI! PEOPLE - Hartford (21 days since its only order), High Profile - Hamden (24 days), and both Shangri-La locations (17 days each). Worth a check-in before they're written off as inactive. Separately, the AR report shows 10 small overdue invoices (~$2,600-$5,100 each) across Crisp, BUDR, Higher Collective - Torrington, and Clone Cannabis - Seymour — nothing alarming individually, but worth a light collections follow-up. On the positive side, Zen Leaf's September invoices are not yet overdue (due 10/3), so payment timing remains on track for now despite the spend shortfall below.

ACCOUNT NOTES — WEEK 3 PACE CHECK

Zen Leaf placed no new orders this week — still stuck at $25,800 across its 6 active locations (Enfield, Meriden, Naugatuck, Newington, Norwich, Waterbury), the same total as two weeks ago, and still well short of the $45k spend needed by month-end to unlock the agreed wholesale cost credits. With roughly 1-2 weeks left in the month, a second round of orders is needed soon or the promo credit threshold will be missed. Crisp and THE LIV - Putnam, both on roughly monthly cadences, had no new orders this week either — consistent with their established patterns, not a new concern.

LOOKING AHEAD: Q4 PRICING RESET AND FIVE NEW WS PRODUCTS LAUNCHING IN OCTOBER

Per the September S&OP review, the Q4 wholesale price list is approved and rolls out on the October menus — a flat, 2x-MSRP price by potency tier that lowers wholesale pricing across most SKUs to drive volume back up (1g Prerolls drop from $6.00 to $4.75, effective immediately; Comffy 1g cart drops from ~$27.50 to $26.00; Aura 1g disposable from $30.00 to $29.00). This directly targets the demand-and-pricing story we've been tracking on Vapes (510) all month, though the new $26-29 wholesale carts still sit above the $40-50/g retail framing used earlier — worth watching how it lands. Five new WS products launch in October: 10g Smalls, Saus singles (a new infused pre-roll line, with multipacks following later in the month), a Comffy 1g cart flavor refresh, Aura 1g carts (new hardware), and Aura 2g disposables. Infused flower (a 952-unit first run) and BiteBack gummies (targeting Black Friday) follow in November and December. The plan's goal is to rebuild wholesale buyer breadth — from 7 active buyer groups this September back toward the ~10 seen at August's peak, reaching roughly 30 of 60 reachable doors by December — and grow wholesale revenue from the ~$227K August/September run-rate to a planned $402K (October), $487K (November), and $592K (December). Worth keeping this pricing and launch calendar in view for the October allocation build, since it directly reshapes several of the categories flagged this month.
