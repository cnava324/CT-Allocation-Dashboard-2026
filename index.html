Section 6 — Strategic Observations

Forward-looking considerations · operations · channel planning · based on Weeks 1–2 of August

AURA DISPOSABLES — INITIAL RESTOCK, 2 OF 6 SKUs LIVE

This week's AURA volume is the first wave of the restock, not the full menu back online — Tahoe OG and Kush Mints (2 of the 6 AURA SKUs) are now Listed to Buyers, with Kush Mints' RFS labeling issue cleared. Both channels moved AURA for the first time all month: WS sold 270u (150u Tahoe OG, 120u Kush Mints) and Retail cleared all 9 stores at 60u each. The remaining 4 SKUs are expected to come online over the next 2–3 weeks, so AURA's MTD attainment (10.8% WS / 22.4% Retail) should keep climbing as more strains land rather than reflect a demand problem.

7g FLOWER FLIPS FROM BALANCED TO OVERSTOCKED

Weeks of Supply jumped from 3.5 to 9.3 — the category crossed the 6-week Overstocked line this week. The driver is supply, not demand: Early Bird 7g landed a new 3,410u batch (on top of the existing 1,397u), and Irish Goodbye appeared in 7g format for the first time (1,208u, new batch) alongside Glow Up's continued 3,338u position. Worth a look at whether 7g production pace needs to ease off given the 3.5g/1g Preroll pattern already showing that flower variety, not volume, is what drives WS pull-through.

1g PREROLLS — OVERSTOCK WORSENS TO 35 WEEKS OF SUPPLY

Already the standing overstock story, this category grew from 27.5 to 35.1 weeks of supply on the back of large restocks across Umami OG (+3,535u), Jai Alai (+3,798u), Super Chill (+1,292u), and Couch Surfer (now 1,085u combined). Demand is healthy in relative terms (128.7% of H2 Forecast MTD), but inventory is growing faster than sell-through can absorb it — same demand-signal story as prior months, just at a larger scale.

MENU CHANGES THIS WEEK — WHAT SOLD OFF, WHAT GOT ADDED

Sold off the menu: Irish Goodbye remains at zero available inventory in both 3.5g Flower and 1g Preroll formats, yet BUDR - Danbury Federal Rd still bought 60u of each this week — meaning demand is outpacing what's technically listed. Worth flagging to cultivation as the strain to prioritize replenishing across those two formats.

Added to the menu: two brand-new entries landed in the 8/12 inventory report. Cloud 9 arrived as a new 3.5g Flower SKU (4,211u, batch created 8/11) — its first appearance in that format. Irish Goodbye also expanded into 7g Flower for the first time (1,208u, batch created 8/11), giving the strain a new format even while its 3.5g and 1g Preroll versions sit at zero. Neither has sold yet, but both add meaningful near-term availability to their categories.

HI! PEOPLE - DERBY AND THE HARVEST CORNER - COLCHESTER — NO ACTIVITY THIS WEEK, DECISION STILL OPEN

Neither account ordered in Week 2, so there's nothing new to weigh — the formal tracked-account decision carried from the July close remains unresolved.

MONTH-TO-DATE PACE CHECK

With 2 of 4 weeks in, WS sits at 57.5% and Retail at 38.8% of their full Aug targets — both still reading below the ~50% pace-line you'd expect at the halfway mark, though WS is closer to on-pace than Retail. Worth revisiting once Week 3 lands to see whether August tracks toward July's ~117% WS / ~114% Retail closing pace.
